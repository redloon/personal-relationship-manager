# Using PRM

## Save a detail

Tap **Add a note** on Home. Write what you want to remember, choose the people it belongs to, and save. You can add someone by name without importing Android Contacts.

Choose **Fact** for a lasting detail about one person, or **Follow-up** for something you want to do. You can also start a note from a person's profile, Android's text-sharing menu, the launcher shortcut, or the Quick Settings tile.

Closing the editor keeps your unfinished draft. **Discard** removes it.

## Find it again

Search Home for a name or a detail you remember, such as “climbing.” Search includes people, notes, facts, tags and follow-ups. Open a person to see their saved context together.

The **People** tab searches names, phone numbers and email addresses. Importing Android Contacts is optional; you can keep profiles that only have a name.

## Follow up when you choose

Create your own follow-ups, with or without a date. Enable optional morning reminders in Settings. Each dated item is notified once; unfinished items do not keep sending reminders. Android may delay delivery.

## Keep a backup

In **Settings → Data**, use **Export data** to save a portable copy, or choose a folder for daily backups. Automatic backup keeps the latest seven verified copies.

Restoring a backup replaces the app's current records after a safety export and confirmation. Android contact links are detached, so you can safely restore on another phone and link people again afterward. Re-enable reminders if you want them; preferences, unfinished drafts and access to backup folders are not restored.

Deleted notes, facts and follow-ups can be recovered for 30 days in Settings. Permanent person deletion has its own confirmation and cannot be undone.
